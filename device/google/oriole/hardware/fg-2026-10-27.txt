    SWEET FG: F2: reading the gauge at I2C 0x36
    SWEET FG: F2 transfer error: INT_STATUS 0x1 TRANS_STATUS 0xa0002 FIFO_STATUS 0x1000001
    SWEET FG: F2 DevName (0x21) failed: transfer error
    SWEET FG: F2 stopped; controller registers restored: CTL 0x48 FIFO_CTL 0x80083 INT_ENABLE 0x80 AUTO_CONF 0x30001 ADDR 0x9400
- my mistake, not the bus: the code treated TRANS_STATUS bit 1 as TRANS_ABORT (the exynos5 layout). INT_STATUS 0x1 (TX_ALMOSTEMPTY) and
  TRANS_STATUS[3:0] = 2 (HSI2C_MASTER_ST_RESTART) with MASTER_BUSY (bit 17) mean the exynos7/autov9 layout: the transfer had just started,
  the register byte still in the TX FIFO (level 1). Also explains ABL's 0x00080001 (state 1 = START) [guess on bit 19].
- fixed in 0157 (second version): INT_STATUS decode only, soft reset + full restore of ABL's setup after any failure.
